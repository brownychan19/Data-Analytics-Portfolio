# Project 1 - Superstore Sales Dashboard using Power BI
A powerful and intuitive data visualization tool built using Power BI. This dashboard offers a comprehensive view of sales data for consumable products, allowing you to quickly and easily analyze performance and identify trends.

[Preview](superstore_dashboard.png)

**Insights**

1. Sales value and profits showed an upward trend each year, with the highest increase occurring in 2018.
2. Sales and profits saw a significant surge in the final quarter (months 9, 11, and 12). The peak occurred in the 11th month (November).
3. The individual retail (consumer) segment is the primary driver of the business. However, the corporate and home office also accounts for a substantial share.
4. California and New York dominate sales volume and profits compared to other states.
5. Phones and Chairs are the two highest-selling products, followed by Storage and Tables.

**Strategic Recommendation**
1. Increase inventory levels (especially for phones and chairs) ahead of October and November to prepare for the peak season.
2. Create a loyalty program or bundle promotion for the Consumer segment to maintain a 55% market share.
3. Optimizing the supply chain to the states that contribute the highest sales, such as California and New York.
